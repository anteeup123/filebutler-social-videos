# FileButler social media videos

Short vertical videos for FileButler in five languages, ready to post on Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts. One file works on all four platforms.

## Layout

- `English/`, `French/`, `German/`, `Portuguese/`, `Spanish/`: nine videos per language, one per topic. These are the posting versions: the narration runs from the first frame and the closing card holds for three seconds after the narration ends (43 to 52 seconds each).
- `<Language>/Full 60 seconds/`: the same nine videos in their original 60-second cut, where the closing card holds in silence until the 60-second mark.
- `Captions/<Language>.md`: a title, caption and hashtags for every topic, in that language, ready to paste.
- `Posting kit/<Language>.md`: the same material written out for each platform (TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn), 45 blocks per language, with the hashtag count each platform allows, "link in bio" where links are not clickable, a cover text for TikTok and Instagram, a first comment for Facebook and LinkedIn, and a founder's opening line for LinkedIn. `Posting kit/posting-kit.csv` holds all 225 posts in one table for a scheduling tool.

## Posting rules the kit follows (checked October 28, 2026)

There is no hidden field on any platform that makes a video get seen. What the platforms read is the caption, the text on screen and the spoken words (TikTok transcribes them), and what decides distribution is whether people keep watching. Every video here opens on the problem in its first second, with burned-in captions, and ends on the trial card.

- TikTok: three to five hashtags in the caption. Links in captions are not clickable, so the kit says "link in bio". Choose the cover frame and type the cover text when you post.
- Instagram Reels: at most five hashtags per caption (Instagram's limit since December 2025; its head has said hashtags describe a post and do not boost reach). No clickable links in captions.
- Facebook Reels: three to five hashtags; put the link in the first comment.
- YouTube Shorts: the title is the hook, the description holds the link, three hashtags show above the title. Hashtags that do not match the video get it removed; more than sixty and all are ignored. A vertical video of three minutes or less is a Short.
- LinkedIn (posted from Joanna's own profile): upload the video file itself, not a YouTube link. The first two lines show before "see more", so the opener carries the point. Three to five hashtags, which are still clickable and searchable. Link in the first comment.

Post one video at a time, one platform account per language, and reply to comments; a company buying a client portal is far more likely to come from LinkedIn and YouTube search than from TikTok.
